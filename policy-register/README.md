# Policy Register

A policy register is an index of obligations and ownership, not a second copy of the policies themselves.

## Fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Policy | The policy or controlled document |
| Owner | One accountable owner |
| Purpose | Why the policy exists |
| Key Obligations | The small number of obligations that matter operationally |
| Evidence / Controls | How the organisation knows the obligations are being followed |
| Review Date | When the policy must be reconsidered |

## Keep obligations operational

Weak:

> Staff must comply with the policy.

Better:

> Two authorised signatories are required for payments above $5,000; the treasurer reviews bank access quarterly.

The register should let a reader understand what compliance looks like without reproducing the full policy.

## Evidence is not aspiration

“Training required” is an obligation.

“Attendance record retained after annual training” is evidence.

Keep the distinction visible.
