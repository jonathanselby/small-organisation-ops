# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial usable release.

- deliberately small project register with five fields;
- cause-event-consequence risk register with controls and residual exposure;
- policy register focused on ownership, obligations and evidence;
- decision register with alternatives, rationale and revisit trigger;
- lightweight operating rhythm for review and archiving;
- coherent fictional community-organisation worked example linking all four registers;
- explicit escalation test for when a simple register is no longer enough.
