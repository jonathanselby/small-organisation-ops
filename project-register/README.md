# Project Register

A deliberately small project register for teams that need one shared view of work but do not need Jira.

## The five fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Project / Workstream | The material piece of work being managed |
| Owner | One accountable person |
| Status | A short, consistently understood state |
| Next Milestone / Date | The next meaningful outcome and when it is due |
| Comments / Actions / Risks | The minimum context needed to intervene or follow through |

The final field is intentionally large. Small operational teams often lose more value by fragmenting information across too many columns than they gain from perfect categorisation.

## Suggested status vocabulary

Keep it small. For example:

- **Green** — on track; no intervention required;
- **Amber** — attention required but recovery is credible;
- **Red** — material intervention or decision required;
- **Complete** — finished and ready to archive;
- **Paused** — deliberately stopped, with reason in comments.

Do not use percentages such as “73% complete” unless the work genuinely supports that precision.

## Good row

| Project / Workstream | Owner | Status | Next Milestone / Date | Comments / Actions / Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hall roof replacement | Priya | Amber | Contractor mobilisation — 18 Nov | Council permit due 12 Nov. R-02 covers weather exposure. Priya to confirm temporary cover by 8 Nov. |

The row says what the work is, who owns it, whether it needs attention, what happens next and why.

## Weak row

| Project / Workstream | Owner | Status | Next Milestone / Date | Comments / Actions / Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roof | Team | In progress | TBD | Working on it. |

Almost every field exists, but the row does not support action.
